## Mechanism estimators and decision criteria

**H18:** primary mechanism is event-minus-ordinary log change of the same ATM call+put premium over five feasible sessions. Fit paired OLS on an intercept, paired synthetic return, paired absolute synthetic return, paired elapsed/DTE fraction and paired entry normalized uncertainty `(C+P)/S/sqrt(DTE/365.25)`. The intercept is a descriptive abnormal-compression estimate, with rank/condition diagnostics and no claim of causal identification or measured IV. The registered direction is negative. Require an upper family-adjusted bound below -0.01 log units and issuer-removal signs remaining negative before calling the mechanism supported. The trade comparison is covered-call net return minus synthetic-long net return at events, less that difference on ordinary days; require a lower adjusted bound above 0.001 stock-notional units, positive doubled-cost mean, and no worse 10th-percentile downside than the matched long baseline.

**H19:** use complete synthetic-price paths independently of future overlay-quote coverage. Early downside is the maximum loss from entry over five sessions; later downside uses the following five sessions relative to that interval's own start. Divide both by entry ATM uncertainty scaled to five sessions with the same predeclared square-root convention; this proxy is a normalization, not a forecast probability. Primary contrast is `(early-late event) - (early-late ordinary)`. Require its lower adjusted bound above 0.10 normalized units, positive leave-one-issuer signs, and no disappearance under extra decision delay. Supplement with a paired regression adding normalized 30-minus-120-day uncertainty to common 120-day uncertainty, past five-session momentum, moneyness and DTE; sparse/rank-deficient fits remain inconclusive. The protective-put objective is a reduction of at least 10% in mean loss among the worst 10% of eligible event returns versus the funded long, with return drag no greater than 0.0025 stock-notional units at filing horizon 5. Compare the protection improvement against ordinary-day protection. Require adjusted interval evidence in the predicted direction and preserve paid insurance on every entered event.

**H20:** a compensation co-tag is not the primary signal. Classify retained text into `verified_incoming_cfo_terms`, `verified_absence_in_complete_filing`, `other_person_or_ambiguous`, and `incomplete_text_unknown`. Verification requires the incoming person's identity and an explicit salary/incentive/equity term in a same-accession passage linked to that person. No sentiment or planned-transition label is inferred. Absence requires a complete filing review; lack of a phrase in an excerpt is unknown. Freeze outcome-blind reviewed classifications and evidence hashes before calculation. Do not fabricate absent classifications when complete documents are unavailable. The primary comparison uses only the first two verified groups, requiring at least eight events and three issuers on each side; otherwise the primary is INCONCLUSIVE and no compensation-filtered strategy is selected. A co-tag and a deterministic text-presence proxy are separately labeled secondary diagnostics.

H20's primary target is five-feasible-session synthetic RMS, regressed on the verified-terms indicator, entry normalized uncertainty, past five-session momentum, concurrent earnings/guidance/debt indicators and issuer effects when estimable. Require the indicator's upper adjusted bound below -0.001 return units and no increased downside magnitude. Its cash-secured-put comparison uses verified terms versus verified absence and ordinary dates, requiring a lower adjusted bound above 0.001 collateral-return units, positive doubled-cost mean, and no worse downside-tail loss. Incoming-CFO identity, missing complete filings or insufficient rank/support prevent a supported conclusion.

Across every primary estimator require at least 12 usable events/pairs, eight issuers, and at least two observations per fitted parameter. Group criteria apply in addition. These are minimum reporting safeguards, not power guarantees. Unavailable intervals, unstable leave-one-issuer signs, concentrated effects, incomplete exposure comparisons and unexplained price discontinuities prevent support. A clearly opposite supported direction or a nonpositive net effect after costs counts against the hypothesis. Wide intervals or missing data remain inconclusive rather than proof of no effect.

The primary family contains three mechanism and three shape-specific comparisons. Use 9,999 deterministic issuer-cluster bootstrap draws, sampling issuer IDs with replacement and retaining multiplicity, with two-sided confidence level `1-.05/6`; seed 20261003. For differences spanning multiple event dates additionally report contiguous 63-session date-block sensitivity with at least four blocks; absent block support prevents a strong replication claim. Secondary diagnostics use 999 issuer draws and pointwise 95% intervals, explicitly nonconfirmatory. Every grid cell, support failure and operational failed attempt is counted in the ledger. Search adjustment addresses this six-comparison round only; it does not repair earlier adaptive discovery.
