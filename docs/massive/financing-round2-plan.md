## Mechanisms and two fixed strategies

Financing disclosures can resolve funding uncertainty while option premiums adjust slowly, or reveal continuing funding risk that makes downside insurance valuable. These are competing interpretations, both registered before trading code. Every hypothesis tests both shapes; no better-looking shape becomes a retrospectively chosen primary.

* **Covered call:** buy 100 actual funded shares and sell one call at approximately 5% above the decision stock price. The resolution mechanism predicts that event-conditioned premium income exceeds lost upside and costs, beyond the same overlay on ordinary dates.
* **Protective put:** buy 100 actual funded shares and one put at approximately 5% below the decision stock price. The funding-risk mechanism predicts a reduction in net downside excursion after every premium and cost, with a bounded average return drag, beyond ordinary-date protection.

The third hypothesis must also compare the combination with each component-only category. A conjunction is not proof that both labels describe the same transaction. The studies overlap; filings cannot be treated as independent copies across studies.

## Cohort, data and availability

Use the supplied static 100-company universe, served 2022-01-01 through 2025-12-31 records, and Massive-only historical market inputs for sponsor replay. Identify filings by normalized CIK and accession. Deduplicate exact source records; keep separate accessions, category hierarchies, supporting excerpts and missing mappings. No excerpt regex admits or rejects the primary cohort. A tag does not prove closing, cash receipt, a facility draw, or favorable news; an absent tag is not proof of absence.

For an issuer with multiple universe share classes, choose the lexicographically first mapped ticker before outcomes; preserve the others in exclusions. Same-issuer disclosures aligned to one decision date become one portfolio decision with all accession IDs, not multiple positions. Repeated labels are recorded without claiming repeated economic transactions. H25 membership requires both labels within one filing, never merely on one calendar date.

Use unadjusted daily stock and option bars for actual share/contract cash accounting, adjusted stock bars for prior features, SPY, Massive splits/dividends and historical contract reference objects. Preserve object SHA-256, request specifications, pagination completeness, retries and denied/missing access. Daily bar closes are price proxies, not verified executable quotes. Historical contract metadata is requested with `as_of` at the decision date, with explicit expiry handling, and complete pagination.

Assumed availability is filing date plus one calendar day, aligned forward to an XNYS session. This is an assumption about historical service delivery, not a verified vendor delivery timestamp. H23/H25 add one session; H24 adds zero. Calculate the signal and contract choice at the resulting session close. Fill at the **next** session close, using its observed price; the execution bar never selects the signal or contract. Extra delay variants add 1, 3 or 5 sessions to the primary delay, then use the same next-close rule. Exchange calendars handle holidays and early closes. Every horizon and cash flow must remain inside 2022–2025; no borrowing 2026 prices.

Primary maturity is closest to 120 calendar days among 90–180 DTE contracts; strike is closest to the 5% OTM target within one percentage point, with ties resolved by earlier expiry, then lower strike, then lexicographic ticker. Call and put are selected independently on the same decision observations. Require a verified 100-share multiplier and no additional deliverables. Unknown or unsupported deliverables remain unsupported, not priced as ordinary contracts. No rolls. Thirty- and sixty-DTE neighbors do not yield post-expiry horizon estimates; those cells are explicitly unavailable.

Entry liquidity gates use only the five completed sessions **before** decision: option volume sum at least 100 and mean stock volume at least 10,000 shares. Missing prior history means unfilled. No entry-bar volume is used as an earlier-known gate. Entry prices must exist on the scheduled fill date. Exit prices are retried on the next five sessions; unresolved positions make full-period performance unavailable. NAV may carry a prior observed mark for one session with a flag; longer missing marks suppress complete-period metrics. Never backward-fill from future data.

Known splits, dividends, and deliverable changes are applied on their dates, with no entry rejection based on a future action. A later unverified adjusted contract stops reliable accounting and is reported unresolved. Funded shares receive observed cash dividends. Short-call assignment is modeled before ex-dividend when the preceding observed call is ITM and time value is less than the dividend; stock is delivered at strike and the call extinguished at the next open. Conservative assignment sensitivity assigns every ITM call before ex-dividend. Assignment is a modeled convention, not an observed exercise record. At expiry use verified stock close and physical strike/intrinsic settlement; expiration must be within the source window. Protective puts are sold at the scheduled exit, with no discretionary early exercise; physical expiry delivery is accounted for when observable.

## Comparisons and estimands

For each event choose one **earlier** ordinary date for the same issuer, 84–252 sessions before the event decision. Ordinary dates must have no served disclosure bundle in their preceding five sessions. Match using prior 21-session stock momentum, RMS return volatility, log mean volume, SPY momentum and SPY volatility. Require 22 price sessions; calculate standardization only from observations strictly preceding the event decision. Maximum absolute standardized feature difference is 2. Minimize squared standardized distance; break ties toward the latest earlier date. Outcomes never choose the date. A missing match remains an exclusion.

For H25 additionally seek debt-only and underwriting-only disclosures from that issuer 84–504 sessions earlier, using the same past-only feature rule. Missing component matches prevent the corresponding interaction claim but do not remove an otherwise eligible event/ordinary comparison. Labels on component-only filings exclude the other component; they need not exclude unrelated labels.

The common eligible unit for a shape is a quartet: event strategy, event stock-only, ordinary strategy, ordinary stock-only, all with complete prices and costs. Compare only on this intersection. Require matched DTE difference at most 30 calendar days, OTM distance difference at most one percentage point, and decision premium/notional ratio within a factor of two. For direct call-versus-put comparisons use the additional intersection eligible for both shapes; never compare different samples without saying so. Report full label coverage, each gate and the matched intersection separately.

The primary exit is 10 trading sessions after entry. Diagnostic unit-trade outcomes at 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 21, 42 and 63 sessions and observed expiry are all retained, including unsupported cells. Net unit returns are P&L divided by entry stock notional; put return drag includes premiums on every entry. Maximum downside excursion uses the minimum daily marked net return including premiums/costs. Downside estimands require complete paired daily mark paths under the one-session carry limit, not merely valid entry/exit bars; missing paths are unsupported and return/downside sample counts may differ. Portfolio metrics use actual cash and NAV, a different denominator.

Let E/O denote event/ordinary; C/P/S denote covered call/protective put/stock-only net returns; D is maximum downside loss, a nonnegative quantity. Covered-call primary estimands are `(C_E-S_E)-(C_O-S_O)` and `C_E-C_O`. Protective-put primary estimands are `(D_S,E-D_P,E)-(D_S,O-D_P,O)`, `D_S,E-D_P,E`, and `P_E-S_E`. For H25 replace O with each component-only comparator for the call overlay and put hedge-benefit contrasts. Record all algebra and signs in exported evidence.

Covered-call development advancement requires a simultaneous lower confidence bound above **0.001** for overlay increment and above zero for strategy increment. Protective-put advancement requires a lower bound above zero for incremental hedge benefit, above **0.0025** for event downside reduction, and above **-0.005** for event return drag. These are practical exploratory criteria, not an alpha guarantee. H25 additionally needs positive simultaneous bounds against both component-only contrasts before claiming an interaction. Each branch can fail independently. A protective-put success is risk protection under a cost budget, not automatically alpha.

Require at least 30 complete pairs across eight issuers overall; a reported annual validation cell needs 10 pairs across five issuers. Otherwise show INCONCLUSIVE/insufficient support. All counts refer to unique issuer/decision units, with repeated matched controls identified. Report 2022 separately as early development and 2023, 2024, 2025 year cells; no hyperparameter selection on those results.

## Portfolio, execution and stress

Each study/shape starts with $1,000,000 cash, zero cash interest, no leverage and one 100-share/one-contract unit. A name is limited to 5% of NAV; funded-share gross and net exposure to 20%. Sector cap is 20%; unknown historical sector mappings share one conservative unknown bucket. Premium cash is not an excuse to exceed funded-share limits. Do not open a second position in the same issuer while one is held. Same-day eligible entries are ordered by normalized CIK/accession, independent of outcomes.

At 5% portfolio drawdown from the high-water mark, request liquidation at the next feasible bar, block entries for 21 sessions, then reset the high-water mark at re-entry eligibility. Record unfilled exits, cooldown, capacity and exposure exclusions. Stock-only counterfactuals use the same accepted entry schedule, sizes, horizon and costs, with their own ledger; ordinary-date portfolios use their own earlier decision schedule and the same constraints. Broad buy-and-hold SPY and same-universe stock comparisons use the same observation dates and cost convention; scale risk using earlier available volatility, not realized future risk.

Per-side costs: stock 6 basis points of traded notional; option adverse friction 5% of premium plus $0.65 per contract. These lump spread/slippage/impact assumptions are deliberately labeled approximations. Doubled-cost sensitivity doubles every stated friction/fee. No borrow or margin financing is incurred by the funded shapes. Quote-supported checks use purchases at ask and sales at bid, plus $0.65 per-option fee and 1% adverse option-premium impact; stock bid/ask fills add 1bp fee plus 1bp adverse impact per side. Doubled quote costs double those fees/impacts while observed bid/ask stays observed. Avoid an extra assumed spread charge.

Acquire a deterministic SHA-ranked maximum of 64 matched pairs per year for bounded quote checks, independent of stock outcomes. Both stock and option quote ages must be at most 60 seconds, timestamps within five seconds, valid positive bid/ask with ask at least bid, displayed size at least one contract, and relative option spread at most 25%. Quotes sampled around the scheduled close must precede the price-decision cutoff for decision inputs; execution quotes are separately tied to the next close. Missing quotes limit executable-price evidence; they do not invalidate the separate bar-proxy cohort or silently become zero spread. Do not claim exact closing execution from a quote many seconds away.

Capacity reports one-contract participation against prior five-session volume, actual execution-day volume as an ex-post capacity diagnostic, stock/option order notional and quoted size where observable. Ten-contract stress uses 1,000 shares, the same capital/limits, and the same per-unit costs; it is a stress scenario, not demonstrated scalable alpha. Intraday capacity is unavailable without appropriate synchronized observations.

Eleven fixed variants per study: primary, doubled costs, extra delays 1/3/5, DTE30/DTE60, OTM3%/OTM10%, conservative assignment, and ten-contract size. Both shapes run for each of the 33 study/variant tasks. Variants are one-at-a-time changes from primary; no hidden Cartesian sweep, winner selection or retuning. Diagnostics at other horizons are additional exposed readings, not untouched portfolio tests.
