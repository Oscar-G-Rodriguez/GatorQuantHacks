# H13: Option-implied uncertainty

Leadership changes may be associated with a different amount of subsequent movement relative to the uncertainty already contained in option premiums.

This is statistical discovery intended to develop a later testable hypothesis. Oscar required this new folder and completed plan to be committed before adding its new data or methods. Read [the full shared plan](../../docs/discovery/options-buffered-plan.md) and [this folder's specification](Discovery%20Plan.md) first. Earlier results are discovery-selected inputs; no independent confirmation or strategy registration is implied.

Historical paired call/put contracts at a shared strike and expiry, selected using information at the pre-filing session. Acquire the 30-, 60- and 120-calendar-day target buckets for every mapped CFO/CEO lead filing and its prior ordinary anchor.

Describe call/put premiums, premium sum relative to parity-derived spot and sqrt-maturity-normalized uncertainty; compare later absolute stock movement and option-premium changes across all eight horizons. These are pricing diagnostics, not implied volatility estimates or option-strategy P&L.
