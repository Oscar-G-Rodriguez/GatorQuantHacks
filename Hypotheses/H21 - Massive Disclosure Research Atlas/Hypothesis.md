## Information and target clocks

Assumed availability is filing date plus one calendar day, aligned to the first eligible XNYS session; this is not verified vendor/publication timing. Inputs are observed at that session's close, with additional whole-session delays 0/1/3/5. Outcomes start after the input close. Required horizons are 1/2/3/5/10/21/42/63 sessions, and every target must finish on or before December 31, 2025. Insufficient warm-up or incomplete subsequent sessions remains missing. No later data completes a target.

Price targets are fractional signed return, absolute signed return, root mean squared subsequent daily returns, and maximum downside excursion `max(0, 1-min(P[t+j]/P[t]))`, j=1..h. Units are fractions, not annualized volatility. Prior controls are returns over 1/5/20/60 sessions, volatility over 20/60 sessions, prior log dollar volume/volume state and SPY returns/volatility. All features use known observations at or before the input close.
