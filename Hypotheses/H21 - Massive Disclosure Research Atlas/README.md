# H21 — Massive Disclosure Research Atlas

H21 is a systematic exploratory atlas of every type in the pinned 119-category Massive disclosure taxonomy. It asks whether labels, same-filing combinations, ordered sequences and supporting excerpts add information about subsequent market outcomes beyond prior prices and option-priced uncertainty. It does not simulate trading or claim alpha.
