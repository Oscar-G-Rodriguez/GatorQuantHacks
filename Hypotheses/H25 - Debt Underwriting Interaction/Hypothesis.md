## 01 — Summary

The same filing can describe both a debt issuance and underwriting arrangements. The study asks whether that conjunction changes the option-overlay result beyond either component category, preserving the distinction between a meaningful interaction and two labels describing ordinary features of one financing.

Test a same-filing conjunction of the served `debt_issuance` and `underwriting_agreement` labels using two complementary funded-share branches: **covered call** and **protective put**. The primary horizon is ten trading sessions and the target option maturity is 120 calendar days. Measure the net event-versus-ordinary effect, the option overlay beyond its own stock leg and the additional information beyond prior market and option conditions. Both branches are predeclared and remain in the evidence family. Neither an attractive stock return nor a single profitable option establishes the proposed contribution.

The verdict at registration is **not yet evaluated**. The eventual paper must distinguish development evidence from fresh confirmation and retain unsupported or unavailable results.

## 02 — Economic Hypothesis

We expect the same-filing debt-issuance and underwriting-agreement conjunction in the supplied 100-company universe to contain information about the next ten sessions of financing risk beyond either component alone because the financing and its distribution arrangements are disclosed together. Placement arrangements may clarify execution exposure while new obligations change equity risk. The proposed opportunity persists if options reflect broad financing uncertainty more quickly than the relationship between the two disclosed components. Co-occurrence does not by itself prove completed funding, new information or an incremental economic interaction.

**Covered-call branch.** An underwriting arrangement accompanying issuance may make placement responsibility and execution exposure more transparent. If options remain priced for uncertainty that has been reduced, a covered-call writer with funded shares could receive an unusual net premium benefit. Call buyers seeking upside or risk transfer are the proposed other side. The prediction must hold beyond debt-only and underwriting-only comparisons; a routine legal-document bundle may simply repeat the same financing information.

**Protective-put branch.** The conjunction can also signal that a financing transaction introduces leverage and distribution-related exposure whose downside is not fully priced. The protective-put branch tests whether purchased protection yields an unusual net increment beyond both the same ordinary-date protection and the component categories. Put sellers and liquidity providers accept the downside risk for premium. Uneven interpretation of the combined financing details is the proposed persistence channel, not proof that underwriting arrangements generally worsen risk.

**Testable prediction.** Each branch tests its separately fixed net event-versus-ordinary contrast and the additional interaction beyond the two components. H25 must report component overlap and the same eligible comparison convention; a positive conjunction return or forecast result alone cannot establish incremental information.

**Falsification.** The interaction mechanism fails if one component, ordinary financing exposure, stock momentum, option prices or redundant labels account for the contrast. It also fails if the result depends on combining labels across different filings, a future-confirmed transaction state or an isolated issuer/year. A sparse component contrast remains insufficient evidence rather than a reason to drop the component comparison.

The two branches do not predict that every financing is simultaneously safe and dangerous. They register distinct option-tail implications for the complete category before new option-program results are inspected. Their counterparty and persistence descriptions are proposed mechanisms, not facts established by these data.

## 06 — Risk Management

Both branches own funded equity risk. The covered call caps upside while retaining substantial stock downside; the protective put pays a premium and supplies protection only under its actual contract, expiry and exercise conditions. Neither branch uses naked option exposure or assumes that a price stop guarantees a fill through a gap.

Use the shared fixed position, issuer/sector, gross/net and overlapping-event limits, funding/cash conventions and de-risking/re-entry policy.
