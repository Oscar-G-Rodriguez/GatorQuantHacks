# H22 registered development backtest

This protocol completes the October 4, 2026 candidate proposal before H22 strategy code and its first backtest. Oscar explicitly requested the HiPerGator backtest after a plain-language explanation. The research question is whether a completed debt financing identifies better times for a funded covered call than comparable ordinary dates. The test can fail. Neither earlier forecast improvements nor a profitable covered call establish this incremental claim.

## Economic prediction and scope

The proposed mechanism is that receipt of financing resolves uncertainty about transaction completion while option premiums still compensate sellers for that uncertainty. Increased leverage, distress and an acquisition funded by debt can produce the opposite effect. We predict a larger net call contribution and a better combined stock/call outcome than matched ordinary periods, rather than predicting positive stock returns alone.

Use Massive API observations for the supplied 100-company universe, January 1, 2022 through December 31, 2025. All of these years are exposed development data; January–August 2026 was also exposed previously. The static universe has survivorship concerns. No available period is being declared independent confirmation. The organizer's sealed period remains untouched and its dates are not invented. A future final-OOS mode must require a frozen package and explicitly attested unseen dates, reserve the shorter of two years or the last 20% of that new history, and refuse dates overlapping the recorded exposed windows. This run is development only.

The H21 source objects, coverage receipt, filings and panel are reused only after SHA-256 verification. The panel's future outcome columns are excluded from H22 inputs. Its adjusted prices can supply strictly prior return/volatility controls; actual stock holdings and strike comparisons use nominal unadjusted prices. Retain raw responses, pagination, failures, empty responses, quote availability and source hashes. Downloads and packaging are local; scientific event selection/matching and numerical backtesting run in scheduled HiPerGator allocations. No paid purchases or non-Massive data enter the notebook or backtest.

## Fixed event coding and clock

Require the exact `debt_issuance` tag. Parse only that tag's supporting excerpt from the raw disclosure record, preserving all other labels for context. An affirmative financing verb (`completed`, `closed`, `consummated`, `issued` or `sold`) referring to notes, bonds, debentures, debt or a debt offering must accompany affirmative receipt of proceeds (`received`, `receipt`, `raised` or `funded`, with `proceeds`, `funding` or `financing`). The evidence may span adjacent sentences in the same excerpt. Future/conditional wording (`will`, `expects`, `intends`, `plans`, `proposed`, `subject to`, `if`, `upon completion`) or negation in the qualifying clauses makes the classification ambiguous unless a separate unqualified completion-and-receipt clause exists. Underwriting or purchase agreements alone do not qualify. No excerpt, insufficient affirmative wording or conflicting evidence remains `unknown`; explicit planned-only text is a negative-control cohort. This is a conservative reproducible text rule, not a verified economic-event ground truth.

Keep classification evidence and a deterministic hash-ranked audit of up to 30 positive and 30 other excerpts. Audit without prices or outcomes; report parser errors and amend prospectively rather than silently recoding after returns. Use the lexicographically first mapped ticker per CIK to avoid multiplying share classes. Keep accession identity. Same-issuer same-date positive filings become one trading signal with all accessions retained. An identical normalized completion excerpt within 63 sessions is a suspected repeated disclosure and produces no new primary entry. Other overlapping signals are logged and skipped while that issuer has an open position.

Assume availability at filing date plus one calendar day, aligned forward to an XNYS session. This is a vendor-clock assumption, not historical delivery evidence. The decision occurs after that session's close; entry occurs at the following session's close. A one-day bar's final close cannot be used to choose a contract and fill it at that same decision close. Contract identity is chosen from the decision-time chain and remains fixed for next-session entry. Extra decision delays of 1, 3 and 5 sessions are separate registered sensitivities. Use the exchange calendar, including early closes, and preserve decision/entry/exit timestamps.

## Instrument, holding and cash accounting

Buy 100 funded shares and sell one standard 100-share same-name American or European call. Select an expiry with 90–180 calendar days remaining at decision, nearest 120 days (ties choose earlier expiry). Within it choose strike nearest 1.05 times nominal decision spot, requiring achieved OTM 4–6% (ties choose lower strike). Reject unknown multiplier, additional deliverables and unsupported exercise style. Selection never uses subsequent contract prices or returns.

Hold for 21 trading sessions from entry. Close the same shares and call; do not roll or select another contract at exit. Record the sponsor horizons 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 21, 42 and 63 as independent fixed-horizon trade diagnostics, not a composite strategy choosing the best exit. Expiry outcomes are recorded only if fully observable inside 2022–2025. The primary portfolio uses 21 only.

For an ordinary close, gross dollars are `100*(stock_exit-stock_entry+call_entry-call_exit)` plus dividends actually accrued during stock ownership. Opening cash is reduced by share purchase and increased by call premium, less friction; closing reverses both legs. Reserve the full stock notional rather than treating the premium as free capital. Keep stock, call, dividend and cost contributions separately. Cash earns zero; no borrow, leverage or credit interest is used. These assumptions are identical across controls.

Primary stage: an **assumed-fill daily-close backtest**, using same-session nominal stock and option trade closes. It is a simulation of an explicit price convention, not executable bid/ask evidence. Require decision and entry option bars and at least 100 contracts' total volume over the previous five sessions, and stock prior-session volume at least 10,000 shares. One contract is at most 1% of this measured five-session option volume; this does not prove intraday capacity. No volume observed after entry establishes eligibility. Missing entry bars mean unfilled. At exit, attempt the scheduled close and then up to five subsequent sessions when both bars exist; preserve delay. A still-unresolved initiated trade is not dropped from results. Mark daily from same-session bars; permit at most one-session carry for NAV with an explicit stale flag. Longer gaps make that portfolio's full-period metrics unavailable. Known unresolved positions prevent a complete-return claim.

Primary assumed friction per side: stock 5 basis points of notional (spread/slippage/impact combined) plus a 1 basis point fee; option 5% of its observed premium (spread/slippage/impact combined) plus $0.65 per contract. These are conservative test conventions, not measured broker costs. Double all friction and fees in a separate scenario. Report break-even added friction and the observed quote spreads, rather than describing this convention as proven realistic execution.

Quote-validation stage: collect bounded last quotes at or before entry and scheduled exit close, within the same session. Require positive bid/ask, bid<=ask, both displayed sizes at least one contract, quote age<=60 seconds, stock/option timestamps within five seconds and option spread<=25% of mid. Execute stock buy at ask / sale at bid and call sale at bid / buy at ask, plus stock 1bp fee +1bp extra adverse impact and option $0.65 +1% of mid adverse impact per side. Double the quoted half-spread plus fees/impact around mid in the doubled-cost scenario, without adding the spread twice. Missing or failing quotes are explicit exclusions for this stage; quote-stage results are conditional on coverage. A bounded sample (up to 64 hash-ranked positive event pairs per year, including their ordinary controls) uses only coverage/identity, never profitable outcomes. Do not generalize this sample into full executable portfolio evidence. This stage can be unsupported without preventing the assumed-fill backtest.

Dividends accrue by ex-date for shares held the previous close and are credited at payment date (or retained as a receivable if payment exceeds the window). For American calls, a modelled early assignment at the following session's open occurs before an ex-dividend date when the preceding observed close is ITM and call time value is less than the declared cash dividend. Assigned stock is sold at strike and call extinguished, with closing fees; report these as modelled assignments, not observed exercise. Repeat assuming assignment at any ITM pre-dividend close as a sensitivity. European calls are not early assigned. At expiry settle at intrinsic with share delivery where appropriate. A split or deliverable change during a held option makes it unresolved unless exact contemporaneous standard deliverables can be verified; do not exclude it at entry using its future occurrence. No future bar or quote repairs a missing past fill.

## Portfolio and controls

Start each comparable portfolio with $1,000,000. One contract per accepted signal; no simultaneous positions in the same issuer. Maximum funded notional 5% per name and 20% gross. Sector history is not verified, so conservatively treat all positions as one unknown-sector bucket capped at 20%; do not fabricate sectors. Net equity exposure is funded and no larger than gross. Sort simultaneous candidates by CIK/accession/ticker. Reject entries violating available cash or these limits; preserve their reasons. At a 5% portfolio drawdown, close at the next feasible session and suspend entries for 21 sessions. Resume after that cooldown with the high-water mark reset to the current NAV; report that operational reset. Gaps can exceed this threshold and exits cannot be guaranteed.

Pair each event decision with one same-issuer earlier ordinary decision 84–252 sessions earlier. Exclude controls having any served filing bundle within the preceding five sessions (absence of a served label is not absence of news). Matching uses adjusted prior 21-session momentum, 21-session RMS volatility, log prior volume ratio to its trailing 21-session mean, SPY 21-session momentum and volatility. Standardize with mean/SD estimated from strictly earlier panel rows only, require every absolute standardized difference<=2, minimize mean squared standardized distance, ties choose the most recent earlier date. No future control date or outcome is eligible. Keep candidates and unavailable matches; no matching on outcome. Acquire contracts under identical maturity/moneyness rules. After acquisition also report achieved DTE, OTM and premium/notional balance; a matched pair enters the primary inference only if DTE difference<=30 days, OTM difference<=1 percentage point and decision premium/notional ratio differs by no more than a factor of two. These option calipers are evaluated before entry and are not adjusted after seeing returns.

Compare event and ordinary covered calls on identical paired eligible trades. Also report unhedged stocks on those exact dates, call-only contribution, cash, and event stock exposure to SPY. A fixed price-only momentum gate admits paired ordinary calls only when prior 21-session stock momentum is positive; a fixed option-richness gate uses decision call premium/notional above the expanding prior-year median of earlier eligible decisions. The latter is unavailable until 20 earlier observations across eight issuers, never a future-fitted threshold. Their returns and coverage are shown separately; sparse gate subsets do not replace the full matched primary comparison. Report event-minus-ordinary stock return as the beta/momentum explanation check, and difference-in-differences of call overlay versus unhedged stock as the incremental overlay test.

## Statistics, fixed variants and acceptance

No parameter optimization is permitted in this run. Chronological readouts are 2022 and expanding prior-year diagnostics evaluating 2023, 2024 and 2025 separately; fitted diagnostic ridge models use only earlier eligible outcomes, purged when a training outcome reaches the validation start. Warm-up needs 21 observed sessions. Register ridge penalty10 for stock/call outcome prediction from prior market variables plus decision premium/notional, comparing the same model with an event indicator. Scaling and gates use training only. There is no claimed untouched IS/OOS table: OOS rows are explicitly unavailable and development tables/curves are separate from any future confirmation.

The primary economic test is mean 21-session event-minus-ordinary covered-call net return, measured on funded stock notional. A practically interesting effect is at least 0.10 percentage point per trade. The second primary test is the paired overlay difference-in-differences. Use 9,999 joint issuer-history and common-calendar-block resamples, year-stratified 63-session blocks, with 126-session blocks as sensitivity; identical resample weights across both primary tests. Use two-sided centered max-t adjustment across these two tests, record zero-variance cells and validate synthetic null calibration. Intervals are exploratory and cannot erase H21/H18–H20 or earlier adaptive exposure. Require at least 30 completed eligible pairs across eight issuers overall and ten pairs across five issuers in each reported validation year. Unsupported cells retain counts and no inferential claim. Report issuer/year concentration and leave-one-issuer-out means.

Fixed strategy neighbors are (DTE target, OTM target) `(30,.05)`, `(60,.05)`, `(120,.03)`, `(120,.10)` in addition to primary `(120,.05)`. DTE ranges are21–45,45–90,90–180; strike tolerances are ±1 percentage point. These four are one-at-a-time checks, not a winner-selection grid. Primary extra delays1/3/5, doubled friction, conservative assignment and sponsor horizons are additional diagnostics. Every task/cell is retained, including empty/unsupported/failed cases. Planned-only debt text and shifted ordinary timing (fixed additional -21 sessions where valid) falsify the claimed completion-specific mechanism. Up to199 issuer/year bundle-preserving timing permutations supply secondary placebo comparisons of the fixed paired outcome, without calling them the still-pending H21 full-search placebos. Secondary comparisons have their own joint max-t family and never substitute for the two primary tests.

`SUPPORTED incremental signal` cannot be returned by this development run because independent confirmation and full feasible execution evidence are missing. Use `INCONCLUSIVE development candidate` if primary thresholds/consistency/mechanism suggest further study but confirmation is missing; `EXPLAINED` if ordinary stock/option exposure accounts for gains; `REJECTED in this development test` if supported evidence contradicts the mechanism; otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE`. No positive net-alpha claim, live order or trading simulation in a sealed period occurs.
