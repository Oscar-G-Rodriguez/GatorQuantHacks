# H12: Buffered disclosure relationships

Leadership and surrounding disclosure categories may describe different later responses when their inputs are delayed by one, three or five sessions.

This is statistical discovery intended to develop a later testable hypothesis. Oscar required this new folder and completed plan to be committed before adding its new data or methods. Read [the full shared plan](../../docs/discovery/options-buffered-plan.md) and [this folder's specification](Discovery%20Plan.md) first. All existing context-covered issuer/session rows; no acquisition is required for this folder. Compare same-filing context, prior-only 5/21/63-session category histories and controls measured 0/1/3/5 sessions before observation.

Pearson and Spearman pairwise input relationships; issuer-adjusted regressions with declared interactions; horizon-by-buffer maps. Known coverage remains separate from absence. Exact same-filing labels are retrospective context, not an earlier-available flag.
