A debt-issuance disclosure supplies information about funding and future obligations. The study asks whether the full standard category helps time an option overlay, without requiring particular completion or cash-receipt words.

## 02 — Economic Hypothesis

We expect debt-issuance disclosures in the supplied 100-company universe to contain information about the next ten sessions of residual financing risk because the disclosed financing terms can change how investors assess funding access, leverage and the distribution of future equity outcomes. The proposed opportunity persists if different participants incorporate those details at different speeds while option premiums still reflect the previous risk assessment. The label alone does not establish completion, receipt of cash, financial health or newly public information.

**Covered-call branch.** A debt announcement can reduce placement or funding uncertainty while call buyers continue paying for upside optionality. The covered-call branch tests whether a funded investor is compensated unusually well for selling that optionality after the assumed disclosure clock, relative to the same option shape on matched ordinary dates. Call buyers seeking leveraged upside or risk transfer are the proposed other side. Their preferences are an economic hypothesis, not observed order-flow evidence.

**Protective-put branch.** New debt can instead increase leverage or reveal financing pressure whose equity downside takes time to be assessed. The protective-put branch tests whether purchasing protection after the same label provides an unusual cost-adjusted incremental benefit relative to ordinary-date protection and the same unhedged shares. Put sellers and liquidity providers accept that downside exposure for the premium. A slower reassessment of residual financing risk is the proposed persistence channel.

**Testable prediction.** The covered-call prediction is a positive net event-versus-ordinary overlay contrast after controlling the stock contribution and ordinary option carry. The protective-put prediction is a positive net protection contrast under its separately fixed criterion. Both branches must be reported; these are complementary tests of different tails, not permission to switch the strategy after seeing a profitable example.

**Falsification.** The financing-information mechanism fails if matched option-priced conditions and ordinary stock/option exposure explain the contrasts, if premiums adjust before feasible entry, or if the net effect disappears under the fixed friction and delay checks. A negative call contrast does not automatically support the put branch, and a beneficial hedge in one loss episode does not establish a beneficial hedge program.

The two branches do not predict that every financing is simultaneously safe and dangerous. They register distinct option-tail implications for the complete category before new option-program results are inspected. Their counterparty and persistence descriptions are proposed mechanisms, not facts established by these data.

## 06 — Risk Management

Both branches own funded equity risk. The covered call caps upside while retaining substantial stock downside; the protective put pays a premium and supplies protection only under its actual contract, expiry and exercise conditions. Neither branch uses naked option exposure or assumes that a price stop guarantees a fill through a gap.

Use the shared fixed position, issuer/sector, gross/net and overlapping-event limits, funding/cash conventions and de-risking/re-entry policy. Preserve the registered handling of corporate actions, dividends, early assignment, expiry, stock halts and unfillable exits.
